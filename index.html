Crossplay Solver

An unofficial word finder for the NYT Crossplay board game.

Click a square to enter a tile already on the board. Type an uppercase letter for a normal tile, a lowercase letter for a tile played from a blank (scores 0). Press Backspace or Delete to clear a square.

Scan a screenshot

Upload one screenshot with both the board and your rack visible (like the in-game view) and it's transcribed automatically — no tapping required. Recognition (OCR) runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Auto-detect didn't work? Scan board/rack separately

Take a screenshot or a straight-on photo, then tap two reference points so the tool knows where the tiles are.
